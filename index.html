How a film gets made

I run the whole cycle alone, and every prompt follows one fixed format: prompt, settings, reference roles and a QA checklist.

  1. 1Script and hookStory beats and pacing built for short-form retention.
  2. 2Character mastersA base face in Midjourney, then reference sheets that lock identity and wardrobe.
  3. 3Shot by shotLens, light and camera move set per shot, then animated and checked for drift and artefacts.
  4. 4Edit and soundCut, grade, music and sound design to the final delivery.
